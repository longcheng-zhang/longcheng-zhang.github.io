Research Fellow @ Nanyang Technological University

Welcome to my homepage! I am Dr. Longcheng Zhang, currently a Research Fellow at Nanyang Technological University.

My research focuses on electrochemical energy and chemical engineering for green fuels and low-carbon chemicals, with particular interests in green hydrogen, ammonia electrosynthesis, and organic electrosynthesis.

I also work on AI-assisted catalyst design, electrochemical operando/in situ Raman spectroscopy, reaction-interface analysis, and the development of membrane-electrode assemblies and reactors.
